# Eggshell: Bombing - Voice Acting Script

You are the voice of every egg! Here is who they are and every line they say.

## How to add your voice
1. Record each line (a phone voice memo app works).
2. Name each file with the line's **ID**, for example `c1_3.mp3` or `c1_3.m4a` (`.wav` and `.ogg` work too).
3. Put all the files in a folder called `voices` next to `EggshellBombing.html`.
4. Play the game. Lines you recorded play your voice; lines you haven't recorded still beep.

## The cast

**NARRATOR:** A wise old golden-crowned egg telling the story. Slow, deep, serious, like a war movie trailer.

**SHELLY (that's you!):** A white egg soldier. Starts as a nervous brand-new Private (high, excited voice) and grows up during the war. By chapter 5 Shelly is older, braver and calmer. Make your voice a little deeper each chapter!

**GENERAL YOLKINGTON:** A big brown egg with a huge white mustache. Old, grumpy and LOUD. Barks every line. Secretly cares, which you only hear at the very end.

**SGT. SCRAMBLE:** A speckled tough-guy egg with a red bandana and a scar. Gravelly, calm, has seen everything. Talks to Shelly like a big brother.

**CPL. BENNY BENEDICT:** A cream egg with glasses. Friendly, nerdy, explains how stuff works. Talks fast when excited.

**MEDIC CODDLE:** A gentle white egg with a red-cross helmet. Kind and calm, even when bombs are falling.

**PVT. POACH:** A tiny blue egg in a helmet that's way too big. Squeaky, nervous, whispers a lot.

**THE ROTTEN EGG:** The villain. A green, stinky egg with an eyepatch and a black cap. Slimy, sneaky, creepy laugh (heh heh heh). Speak slow and low.
