# Eggshell: Bombing - Voice Script

Every line is already spoken by the computer, and each egg has its own voice (pitch, speed and a different built-in voice). Turn them off on the title screen with **VOICES: ON/OFF**.

## Want to use your own voice for a line?
1. Record it (a phone voice memo app works).
2. Name the file with the line's **ID**, for example `c1_3.mp3` (`.m4a`, `.wav` and `.ogg` work too).
3. Put it in a folder called `voices` next to `EggshellBombing.html`.

Your recording replaces the computer voice for that line only. Every other line keeps the computer voice.

## The cast

**NARRATOR:** A wise old golden-crowned egg telling the story. Deep, slow and serious, like a war movie trailer. Computer voice: very low and slow.

**SHELLY:** The hero, a white egg soldier. Starts as a nervous new Private with a high, excited voice, and gets a little deeper every chapter as Shelly grows up.

**GENERAL YOLKINGTON:** A big brown egg with a huge white mustache. Old, grumpy and LOUD. Secretly cares, which you only hear at the very end.

**SGT. SCRAMBLE:** A speckled tough egg with a red bandana and a scar. Gravelly and calm, talks to Shelly like a big brother.

**CPL. BENNY BENEDICT:** A cream egg with glasses. Friendly and nerdy, talks fast when excited.

**MEDIC CODDLE:** A gentle white egg with a red-cross helmet. Kind and calm, even under bombs.

**PVT. POACH:** A tiny blue egg in a helmet that's way too big. Squeaky and nervous, whispers a lot.

**THE ROTTEN EGG:** The villain. A green, stinky egg with an eyepatch. The lowest, slowest, creepiest voice of all.
