SURVIVE THE NIGHT IN THE LIBRARY
Headphones recommended. Very scary.

HOW TO PLAY

You fell asleep reading and got locked in the library. Survive from 12 AM to 6 AM (5 minutes).

MR. PAGES stands on his podium... until you look away. He sees and hears you. Running is loud. He can't climb stairs. If he sees you hide, he'll check that spot!
THE BOOKWORM shoots down hallways if you stay in one too long. Crouch in hallways!
THE BOOK BATS swarm your flashlight. Turn it off to lose them.
THE BOOKMARKS trip you. Mash to break free!
Your flashlight battery never refills. Hide under desks, in cabinets and in shelf gaps, but after 20 seconds you start breathing loudly.

KEYS: WASD move · Mouse look · Shift run · C crouch · F flashlight · E hide/use · Esc pause
ARROWS mode: ↑↓ walk · ←→ turn · , . sidestep
PHONE: left joystick · swipe right side to look · buttons
