How to play

Keyboard and mouse

Move with WASD or the arrow keys. Aim with the mouse and click (or hold) to attack. Space fires your Super, Q uses your gadget (Power 7+) and F activates Hypercharge (Power 11). P or Esc pauses.

Touch, iPad and tablets

Put your left thumb anywhere on the left half to move. Tap the big red button to auto-aim at the closest enemy, or drag from it to aim yourself and let go to fire. The yellow Super button works the same way once it is charged. The small green button is your gadget and the purple one is Hypercharge.

Tips

Each attack uses one of the three orange ammo bars, which refill on their own. Landing hits charges your Super. Hide in tall grass to vanish from enemies, until you shoot or they walk close. Stay out of fights for three seconds to heal.
