    Sneaky Bugs

    You are a tiny helper program inside Claude's computer, building a video game. But some helpers are secretly Bugs trying to crash everything. Finish the game, or find the Bugs and vote them out!

    The 12 roles

    How to play

    • Move with WASD or the arrow keys. On a touchscreen, use the joystick.
    • Helpers walk to the glowing yellow stations and press E to do a job. Each job is a mini-game. Fill the bar to 100% and the helpers win!
    • Bugs pretend to work. They zap helpers with Q, break things with X, and sneak through cable ports with V.
    • Found a crashed helper? Press R to report it. Everyone meets, talks, and votes.
    • You can also ring the bell in the CPU Core to call a meeting (once per game).
    • Special roles use their power with F. Press M for the map.
    • If something breaks (lights, fan, network), follow the red arrow and fix it fast!
