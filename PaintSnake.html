Paint Snake

Slither, paint, and loop around things to catch them. Mix colors to catch the tricky ones!

How to play

👆Put your finger down anywhere and drag the way you want the snake to go. (Arrow keys work too.) 🎨Your tail paints a stripe. Draw a loop and touch your own paint to SPLAT the inside full of color. 🍎Things inside the loop get caught, but only if your paint is THEIR color. 🪣Slither over a paint pot to add its color. The puddle washes you clean. 🧽Erasers munch your paint and bonk you. Trap them in a loop to pop them!
