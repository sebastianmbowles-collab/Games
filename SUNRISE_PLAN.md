# Sunrise: game plan

Built from 100 questions. The game is `Sunrise.html`.

## Story
- Pip is a classic orange fox. Pip's forest is stuck in endless night.
- Long ago the Sun Stone (a golden orb) fell into an ancient temple. Nobody stole it.
- Pip's father left (it is never explained why). Pip's mother went into the temple to help,
  and Professor Feathers the owl helped her. She gave her own light to keep the Sun Stone glowing.
- Levels 1-3: Pip travels through the dark forest to reach the temple.
- Levels 4-10: inside the temple. Level 10 has the Sun Stone locked in a vault.
- A short cutscene shows Pip carrying the stone back to the forest's biggest tree. The sun rises.
- Sad ending: Professor Feathers, normally long-winded, says only "Thank you, Pip." and flies away for good.
- 3 secret mural rooms tell the backstory. Finding all 3 adds an extra final scene:
  a small fox-shaped light glowing inside the Sun Stone. No twist.
- Short intro scene at the start. Title screen shows the dark forest.

## Pip
- Side-on view. Quick and nimble. Jumps 2 blocks high. Climbs vines. Swims slowly.
- Digs straight down through soft dirt only. Dug dirt comes back only when the room restarts.
- Pushes and pulls blocks (hold G). Can stand on blocks. Falling blocks can crush Pip.
- Only makes fox sounds, no words.

## Controls (computer browser)
- Arrow keys or WASD: move. Up/W or Space: jump. Up on a vine: climb. Down/S: dig.
- G: grab block / spin a vine tile. H: call Professor Feathers for a hint (5 coins).
- P or Esc: pause. M: mute.

## Puzzles (mixed together in each level)
- Gem blocks, 4 colors. Line up 3 of the same color and they vanish.
- Vine-bridge tiles. Stand next to one and press G to spin it.
- Symbol locks. Push sun, moon, star and leaf blocks into slots in the right order.
- Blocks are affected by gravity, and all blocks float in water.

## Danger
- Stone golems (mossy rock, glowing eyes) walk back and forth. They can't be beaten.
  They fall into dug holes and get stuck.
- Traps: spikes, bottomless pits, falling rocks, dart shooters.
- 3 lives for the whole game. Restarting a room costs a life.
- Hidden glowing berries give extra lives.
- Out of lives: back to the last checkpoint (levels 1, 4, 7, 10). The game saves.
- One difficulty. Levels slowly get harder. No boss. No cheat codes.

## Collecting
- Ancient coins, spent on hints (5 coins each).
- Nothing is buried in dirt. Secret areas hold the murals.

## Look and sound
- Pixel art. Levels are 4 screens wide and scroll.
- Forest (1-3), then temple zones: mossy halls, flooded caves, golden sanctum.
- Dim, with glowing moss. Animated fireflies, dripping water, swaying vines.
- Each level ends by lighting a torch. The room brightens.
- Top of screen: lives, coins, level number.
- Calm music. Sound effects for jump/dig, coins/berries, traps, puzzle solved.

## Build steps
1. Pip moving, jumping, digging and climbing in level 1. Movement feel matters most.
2. Blocks, gems, vine tiles, symbol locks.
3. Golems, traps, lives, checkpoints, saving.
4. All 10 levels.
5. Professor Feathers, hints, murals, intro, ending.
6. Music and sound.
